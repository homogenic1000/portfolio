Korg e-LIVEsEx
JEWEL CASE

This is projet is an hommage for Nelson Weber, a great friend of mine and also a great compositor. It's a cd book and jewel casve i've designed, the track name is Korg e-LIVEsEx it's mechanical, metallic mysterious, hypnotisyng

INSIDE THE KORG

The inside of the cd book i've used the JetBrains Mono fontface, it represent exactly the genre of the track ; nerdy, electronic, complex. It's also a great open-source type that i personnly love. The clusterd line is a photo of the EMX-1 that i vectorized it'sa reference to how this track was made

BACK OF THE KORG

The visual at the back come from the manual of the Korg EMX-1, it's the routing of the sequencer/synth used to make this song. The logo at the bottom left "DBOZO RECORDS" doesn't really exist it's a reference of the old name of a soundsystem nelson is part of.

It's a really personnal project i've made specially for Nelson, it's an homage to our friendship, the track is responsible for our encouter and made me discover kinda of introduce me to more deep and niche electronic music.

Thank you Nelson
